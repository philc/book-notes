Book Notes

The Man's Guide to Women - John Gottman and Julie Schwartz Gottman

Gems

  • "For women, being sexual is equivalent to being vulnerable." And a woman will not be vulnerable if she feels at risk of physical or emotional harm.
  • "People like people who like them."
  • "Unlike men, women are much more geared for collaboration, not competition."
    • Understand her opposing viewpoints, rather than trying to win.
  • "A woman's desire is dependent on her feeling desirable."
  • "When you find 'the one,' you cherish her and you express gratitude for what she adds to your life. You don't compare your relationship with her to what you might be missing out on by being committed. It's more than just trust and loyalty, it's an epic journey — the Hero's journey."

What do women really want? (chap 1)

  • They want trustworthiness (doing what you say) because it provides her safety and well-being. She wants to know you will safeguard her heart.
    • Firefighters are a symbol of this trait. This may be why they're considered so attractive.
  • "The first complaint is: 'He is never there for me.' The second complaint is: 'There isn't enough intimacy and connection.' These women feel alone even when they are in a relationship."
    • By contrast, men often feel alone when there's fighting and not enough physical intimacy.
  • The solution is attunement, rather than trying to fix the emotions.
  • How to attune
    • Physically turn towards your partner.
    • Understand, ask questions. Don't problem solve.
    • Empathize
      • "The emotional part of the brain calms down when it feels connected to another person and not alone."
      • If her emotions are about the man's behavior, conflict resolution will go better after attuning, and attuning doesn't mean agreeing with the criticisms.
    • Listen
      • Listen rather than try to impress. "Listening is sexier than talking."
  • If you use your loud voice or large size to intimidate, it will break the trust and her feeling of physical safety.
  • "For women, being sexual is equivalent to being vulnerable." And a woman will not be vulnerable if she feels at risk of physical or emotional harm.

A mind of her own (chap 2)

  • (Understanding the mind of a woman)
  • Unlike men, female hormones rise and fall each month.
    • First two weeks of menstrual cycle: estrogen is peaking. "Women are friendlier, more talkative, more socially relaxed, calmer."
    • Second two weeks: progesterone is peaking. "Women can be more irritable, more stressed, and less calm, plus they want to be alone more."
    • "What you need to remember is that the woman you were in a relationship with last week might not be the woman you are in a relationship with this week."
  • Men have 10-100x more testosterone.
  • "In men, the brain centers for sex and for vision are closely linked, which is why just looking at women turns men on."
  • Men don't generally feel unsafe; most women do.
    • At a therapy workshop, the host asked a crowd to raise their hands if they feared for their life in the last week. Every female hand went up. (That's wild.)
    • Anecdote: someone violently cuts you off while driving, with your wife in the passenger seat. The wife is alarmed by how close you came to a crash. Rather than getting angry at the other driver, "a hero, realizing a woman's response to being startled is quite different than his own, backs off from the other driver and turns his attention to making the woman next to him feel safe."
    • Walk behind her, to protect her.

Read my hips (chap 3)

  • (What women are attracted to)
  • "Women are attracted to the expertise you demonstrate in your particular area of interest." (social status)
  • He posits that women send "I'm open to you" signals first, and then men approach, not the other way around. Rejection happens when men ignore those signals and approach.
    • (This chapter includes a long catalog of signals that women send when they're flirting, many of them unconscious).
  • Facial symmetry is generally attractive.
  • When a man takes up more physical space it, signals an open body posture and social status. High social status is attractive.
  • Stress is not sexy. Calm is.

First impressions (chap 4)

  • "If the work questions seem like they are going nowhere or are not inspiring her to tell you about herself, you can ask another open-ended question: What do you love to do when you are not working?'"
  • Research shows that "lower-pitched voices in men are considered more attractive to women, and also that men with deep voices have more children."
  • "People like people who like them."
    • Giving one's full attention and eye contact signals that you like them.
  • "Research shows greater compliance to a request if you have 'light tactile contact.' So touch her forearm for 1 to 2 seconds while you are asking for a dance."
  • "Walk on the busy side of the sidewalk, walk in stride with her, demonstrate your protective instincts. Ask yourself how a Hero would act."
  • "Unlike men, women are much more geared for collaboration, not competition."
    • Understand her opposing viewpoints, rather than trying to win.
  • "Don't talk about ex-girlfriends. Don't tell stories that turn you into a victim. Talk about who you are and what you think about where you are going in life."

Making your move (chap 5)

  • (On seduction).
  • "For every individual, there's a percentage of people who are drawn to their pheromones and a percentage who are not. There's nothing you can do about this — it's chemistry."
  • Women generally have a stronger sense of smell than men.
  • Women smell the heads of babies because they emit pheromones that that produce oxytocin, which is "responsible for feelings of love and intimacy and pleasure."
  • First kiss
    • "It matters a lot. She will remember everything."
    • "66 percent of women say they have ended a relationship because of a kiss that did not go well."
    • The lips have a large representation in the brain's cortex; there's lots of sensation. Stimulation "releases a flood of feel-good hormones."
    • The book's advice is "don't ask to kiss her." Just do it.
  • Hovering: "Anticipation that something wonderful is about to happen releases dopamine and makes the actual experience even more pleasurable."
  • Build anticipation for the kiss throughout the date. Don't move in too fast.
  • Mistake: "You treat a first kiss like it's a means to an end, rather than an important and memorable experience."

Is she more than a hookup? (chap 6)

  • It's hard to reason about the relationship shortly after physical intimacy.
    • "Oxytocin makes you feel great, but it not only clouds your judgment — it can shut down the fear response in your brain."
  • Limerence: the feeling of lust, desire, and falling in love early in the relationship. It's euphoric, but makes you less cautious and observant of warning signs. "It's like being high on love."
  • "People don't change. That's right. At their core, who they are and how they treat others will not change. This is true for men and for women. So pay attention to the red flags popping up around her — you know, the ones you're ignoring because you're in limerence." (Strong assertion)
  • Watch closely how she treats her pets, friends, family; that's how she will treat you. Is she dismissive; self-centered?
  • Heart: is she kind? Does she blame others? Is she good with secrets? Is she negative by default?
  • Mind: does she show integrity, have follow-through; is she accepting of your friends?"
  • Body: not every woman will set off the cascade of hormones that makes up limerence. "Save yourself the time and energy — biology is a powerful thing that does not respond to manipulation." If you know you're not strongly attracted to each other, don't force it.

Image is everything (chap 7)

  • (How women view their bodies)
  • The man should tell her how great she looks under every condition and lighting, as one admires fine art.
  • "Most women have been taught from an early age that their physical attractiveness is their greatest asset and their most intrinsic way of pleasing others and getting love. A woman doesn't see herself separately from her body, and therefore she often defines her worth in terms of her body."
    • The conflict comes from however much distance she has from society's perception of beauty, primarily conveyed through advertising.
  • Do not criticize or joke about her looks. It will take thousands of compliments to repair that.
  • "You can help her feel beautiful in the mirror of your eyes."
  • Prior to modern beauty standards, "curvy" was the standard.
    • "When a society is well fed, thinness denotes self-control or an ethereal abstinence that appears saintly. So thinness became chic in the 1920s, when the industrial revolution was booming and the middle class was well fed and growing."
  • "A woman's desire is dependent on her feeling desirable."
    • She has to be feeling good about her own body.
    • "The most important part of being a great lover, of being her best ever, is letting her feel your loving appreciation for her body in and out of bed."

Her body is a wonderland (chap 8)

  • (How to navigate the bed room)
  • Her "capacity for ecstasy always begins in a woman's mind and heart. They are her most powerful erogenous zones." The man must first attune to turn her on.

Becoming her best ever (chap 9)

  • Author's claim: women have more prerequisites for sex than men. Women need to feel connected first.
  • In couples — married or unmarried — expect that a partner will respond to overtures for sex 75% of the time.
  • Anticipation releases dopamine. So does novelty. "The more you keep things interesting and avoid pushing the familiar buttons, the better."
  • Don't necessarily get locked into the same routine of progressing through the typical body parts. Explore all over her body and see which areas can cause her breathing to get heavy.
  • "Allow yourself to be turned on by her arousal, rather than just your own arousal."
  • "The average refractory period for all men is typically a half hour."
  • A hero holds her after sex is over. "This is a great source of pleasure for women… it will go a long way toward making you her best ever."

Learn to fight like a girl (chap 10)

  • In conflict, women prioritize being better understood by their partners. Men prioritize problem solving.
  • Avoid the four horsemen, which escalate the conflict: criticism, defensiveness, contempt, stonewalling.
  • What to do when flooded: breathe deep, count to ten. This self-soothes and lowers adrenaline. The last resort is to take a break, which is better than storming out.
  • How to take a break when flooded
    • "I'm having a hard time listening to you right now, and I will come back in 30 minutes so we can continue to talk."
    • Breaks need to be 20 minutes long to diffuse stress hormones.

Best friends forever (chap 12)

  • Women seek more social support from their friends than do men. That's why they "travel in packs."
  • Men tend to confide mostly in their spouse. Women rely on a larger and more intricate network of friends for this, in addition to their spouse. They're more socially connected.
  • He should see her friends as an asset to him, because they provide to her listening support and help to handle her big emotions.

Is she the one? (chap 13)

  • (About commitment, and phases of love)
  • Stage 1: limerence.
  • Stage 2, post-limerence: "During the second stage of love, you start to come down from this hormonal love drug and the haze begins to clear. You start to see the red flags that you ignored before. Stage two is all about building trust."
  • Stage 3, commitment, building loyalty: "Couples in stage three of love, we have found, always seem to feel there is a purpose to their relationship — that together they have created something bigger than themselves. And when these couples talk about meeting, falling in love, and choosing to be committed to each other, there are always expressions of fondness and admiration."
  • The right woman for you should feel like a warm bed fitted just for you when you're around her. If it's an icy bed with scratchy sheets (due to negativity), she's probably not the one. "A good relationship is not one where the negativity only increases."
  • Shared hobbies are not important. It's important how you are when you're together. And that you have the same approach to feelings about feelings.
  • The authors argue that investing in a fulfilling long-term romantic partnership is the biggest factor in health and happiness. Live longer.
  • "When you find 'the one,' you cherish her and you express gratitude for what she adds to your life. You don't compare your relationship with her to what you might be missing out on by being committed. It's more than just trust and loyalty, it's an epic journey — the Hero's journey."

Staying together (chap 15)

  • "Be endlessly curious." "Never stop dating her. Never stop getting to know her."
    • "What does she dream about? What and who are most important in her life right now? what is she afraid of? What is she worried about? … What is her biggest struggle?"
  • Be careful that the woman doesn't feel lonely in her relationship due to a lack of support or intimacy.
  • "You may struggle at times to understand women, but understanding them, and loving them, is one of the greatest adventures life has to offer."
